# Senders

Who is who in your mail, so your assistant knows what can be filed and what must reach you.
One row per sender or domain. Tiers:

- **leadership**: never filed as done without you.
- **always you**: comes to you every time (for example a key customer or your manager).
- **routine**: notifications, receipts, newsletters; can be filed as done once read.
- **ignore unless copied**: group lists you only care about when you are named.
